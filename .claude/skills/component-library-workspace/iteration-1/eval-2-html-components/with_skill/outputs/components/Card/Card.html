Elevated Card

With a subtle drop shadow

This is the default card style. It features a medium box-shadow that intensifies on hover to provide depth and visual feedback.

Outlined Card

Bordered, no shadow

A lightweight card with a visible border. Ideal for dashboard widgets and list items where heavy shadows would be distracting.

Flat Card

A minimal card with no border or shadow — just a subtle background tint. Great for information sections or inline content blocks.

Card with Image

A media slot at the top

Cards can include a top-level image via the .card__media slot.

Interactive Card

Entire card is clickable

Wrapping the card in an <a> tag makes the whole surface clickable while keeping it keyboard-accessible.
