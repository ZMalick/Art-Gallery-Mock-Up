# Kay's Originals

## Welcome

Kay's Originals is the public website for your gallery in Austin, Texas. It introduces visitors to your contemporary artists on consignment and your Permanent Collection, lets them browse every piece in the gallery, learn about each artist, and discover upcoming and past exhibitions.

The site is informational. No shopping cart, no checkout, no transactions take place on the site itself. Prices appear as informational labels on contemporary consignment work where you have approved them. Permanent Collection pieces never display prices.

The site is built for two audiences:

1. Customers, who browse artwork, learn about artists, and find the gallery.
2. Artists, who learn about consignment and apply to show their work.

This manual walks through every page on the site. Each section explains the page's purpose and how to use it.

---

## How To Open The Site

Open any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) and go to the address bar. The site loads at the homepage.

Every page on the site shares a sticky navigation bar at the top with these links:

**Artwork | Artists | Exhibitions | About | For Artists | Contact**

The Artwork link is also a dropdown. Hovering it shows shortcuts to filter the gallery by All, Paintings, Sculptures, or Sketches.

On a phone or tablet, the navigation collapses into a hamburger menu (three lines) in the top-right corner. Tapping it opens the same links.

## 1. Home

**Purpose:** The first page a visitor sees. It introduces the gallery, highlights a featured piece of artwork, shows a photograph of the gallery space, and provides a quick path to the rest of the site.

**How to use:**

1. Open the site. The homepage loads automatically.
2. The hero section at the top shows a featured artwork on a rotating slideshow. Each image stays for ten seconds and crossfades into the next.
3. The "Find Us" area below the hero shows a photograph of the gallery and a Google Maps link. Clicking the address opens directions in a new tab.
4. Scroll down to see featured artists, a preview of current exhibitions, and a closing call to action.
5. Use the navigation at the top to jump to any other section of the site.

## 2. Artwork (Gallery)

**Purpose:** The main browsing page. Every piece in the gallery, contemporary and Permanent Collection, lives here.

**How to use:**

1. From any page, click **Artwork** in the navigation, or use the dropdown to jump to a specific category.
2. The page loads with all 21 pieces visible.
3. At the top of the page, the filter pills let visitors narrow by category: **All, Paintings, Sculptures, Sketches**. Clicking a pill instantly filters the grid.
4. The Artist and Decade dropdowns let visitors filter further. Selecting an artist or decade narrows the grid in real time.
5. Each card shows a thumbnail, title, artist name, and (where applicable) price.
6. Clicking any card opens that piece's full detail page.

[Screenshot: Artwork page with filter pills at top and grid of pieces below]

## 3. Artists

**Purpose:** A directory of every artist represented by the gallery, including both contemporary consigning artists and artists in the Permanent Collection.

**How to use:**

1. From the navigation, click **Artists**.
2. The page shows a grid of 10 artists, each with a portrait, name, and short tagline.
3. Clicking an artist card opens their full Artist Detail page.

## 4. Artist Detail

**Purpose:** The full profile for any single artist. Shows their bio, the works the gallery represents, and the exhibitions they have been part of.

**How to use:**

1. From the Artists page, click any artist card.
2. The Artist Detail page opens with the artist's portrait, name, hometown, and biography.
3. Scroll to see the artist's works in the gallery. Each piece is a card that links to its Artwork Detail page.
4. Below that, any exhibitions the artist has appeared in are listed. Each exhibition card links to that exhibition's detail page.

[Screenshot: Artist Detail page with bio, works, and exhibitions sections]

## 5. Artwork Detail

**Purpose:** The full detail page for any single piece in the gallery.

**How to use:**

1. From the Artwork page or any artist's profile, click a piece.
2. The detail page opens with a large image of the work.
3. Below the image are: title, artist name (linked to the artist's profile), year, medium, dimensions, and price (if the piece is contemporary consignment work that you have approved a price for).
4. A description of the piece sits below the metadata.
5. At the bottom, a "Related Works" section shows other pieces by the same artist or in the same category.

[Screenshot: Artwork Detail page with large image and metadata]

3. Each exhibition card shows a featured image, title, dates, and a one-sentence description.

## 7. Exhibition Detail

**Purpose:** The full page for a single exhibition, with the featured artwork, a written description, and the participating artists.

**How to use:**

1. From the Exhibitions page, click any exhibition card.
2. The detail page opens with the exhibition's title, dates, and a hero image.
3. The body explains the theme and any opening event details.
4. Below, the participating artists are listed. Each links to their Artist Detail page.
5. The pieces featured in the exhibition appear at the bottom. Each card links to its Artwork Detail page.

**How to use:**

## 10. FAQ

**Purpose:** Answers to the questions visitors and artists ask most often.

**How to use:**

1. From the navigation, scroll to FAQ in the footer, or follow links from the For Artists or Contact pages.
2. The page is split into two sections: Questions for Visitors and Questions for Artists.
3. Each question is collapsible. Clicking a question expands the answer. Clicking it again collapses it.

## 11. Contact

**Purpose:** Two separate forms, one for customers and one for artists, so you can route inquiries cleanly.

**How to use:**

1. From the navigation, click **Contact**.
2. Choose the form that matches the inquiry: customer questions or artist inquiries.
3. Each form asks for name, email, subject, and message.
4. The page also lists the gallery's address, phone, and hours.

[Screenshot: Contact page with both forms]

## 12. Artist Login

**Purpose:** A login page for artists who already have an account with the gallery. The login is currently a user-interface placeholder. There is no backend account system on the live site yet.

**How to use:**

1. From the For Artists page or the footer, click **Artist Login**.
2. Enter an email and password.
3. Clicking "Log In" submits the form. Because there is no backend yet, the form is for demonstration only.
4. Artists who do not have an account can click "Apply to Consign" to go to the Artist Signup page.

[Screenshot: Artist Login page]

## 13. Artist Signup

**Purpose:** The application form for new consigning artists.

**How to use:**

1. From the For Artists page, click "Apply to Consign". From the Artist Login page, click the signup link.
2. Fill in the form: name, email, phone, portfolio link, medium, and a short statement about the artist's work.
3. Click submit. As with Artist Login, the form is currently a user-interface placeholder until a backend is connected.

[Screenshot: Artist Signup form]

## 14. Page Not Found (404)

**Purpose:** A friendly page that shows up when a visitor lands on a URL that does not exist.

**How to use:**

1. The page appears automatically if a link is broken or a visitor types a wrong URL.
2. The page shows a clear message: "Page Not Found".
3. A button labeled "Back to Gallery" returns the visitor to the homepage.

## What To Do When You Want Something Updated

The site content (artworks, artists, prices, exhibitions, gallery photos, hours, address) lives in two places:

1. **Text and data:** in the file `js/data.js`. Changes to artwork titles, prices, descriptions, or artist information happen here.
2. **Images:** in the `images/` folder. New artwork photos or artist portraits go here.

Send updates to the development team and the team will apply the change. The team's contact information is on the cover page of this manual.

---

## Closing

The site is designed to grow with the gallery. Adding a new artist, a new piece, a new exhibition, or updating prices is a content change, not a structural change. The structure stays consistent across every page so visitors always know where they are and how to get back to where they came from.

Thank you for trusting our team with the build.
